Eternal Amaris is the connective tissue between the games. It exists so that
projects made years apart, in different genres and different engines, can
still be part of one thing.

It is being written here, in public, as the games are made. This page is the
first entry rather than a summary of a finished bible — the setting grows
when a game needs it to, not before.

## What holds so far

The line-art cosmos in the studio's identity is not decoration: rings,
orbital paths and the flying sword are how the setting reads from the
outside. Cultivation sits next to the mundane without either one winning.

Everything below this line is written as it becomes true in a shipped game.
