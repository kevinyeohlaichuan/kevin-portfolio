*Amaris* is the gift. *Eternal* is the problem. Something divine gave this world
a thing it never asked to keep, and the people living in it have spent every
generation since deciding what that means.

## Five bloodlines, and everything between

The setting runs on five strands rather than a species list:

- **人 · Human** — no inheritance, no advantage, and the shortest patience.
- **魔 · Demon** — appetite as a birthright, which is not the same as evil.
- **仙 · Immortal** — cultivation, ascension, and the long arrogance that comes with time.
- **妖 · Spirit** — the wild that learned to wear a face.
- **机械 · Machine** — built, not born, and increasingly unwilling to accept the difference.

None of these stay pure. They cross. A half-machine, a demon wearing steel, a
cultivator with spirit blood, someone carrying all five at once — the crossings
are the interesting part, and most of the world's arguments are about them.

Above all of it sits the divine, and whether it counts as a bloodline at all is
one of the questions the games are for.

## The tone

Adult, and meant that way. Violence is real and has a cost. So is tenderness —
people love each other here, and the story does not flinch from that or leer at
it. It sits in the middle on purpose: not a comfort fantasy, not a misery
contest.

## Where it comes from

Myth from many places, taken apart and rebuilt. Cultivators sit beside things
that came from somewhere else entirely. No real nation is named or claimed —
the references are raw material, not a map.

## What is settled

Not much, deliberately. Entries appear on this page when a game has made them
true, and not one line before. Everything above is the shape of the thing; the
detail is being written in the projects themselves.
