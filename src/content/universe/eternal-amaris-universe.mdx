Eternal Amaris is still cooking. It may connect games made years apart, in
different genres and different engines, but the recipe is not ready yet.

For now, this is a small sign on the kitchen door: work in progress. New lore
will appear when a game makes it true.

## What is on the table

The line-art cosmos in the studio's identity is not decoration: rings,
orbital paths and the flying sword are how the setting reads from the
outside. Cultivation sits next to the mundane without either one winning.

Nothing here is final until it appears in a game.
