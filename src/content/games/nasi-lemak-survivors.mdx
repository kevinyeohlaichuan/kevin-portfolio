A nasi lemak aunty defends her stall by tossing food at incoming customers.
Once their satisfaction bar is full, they leave. Nasi lemak biasa deals
single-target damage, nasi lemak berapi bursts across an area, and nasi lemak
rendang pierces through a line of customers.

Active recipes and supporting passives can level without a fixed cap. When a
matching active and passive reach the right point, the recipe evolves in the
style of a survivor roguelite. A run records the highest wave, survival time
and player name.

Built solo: gameplay systems, pixel art, animation, balancing, store
listing and release.
