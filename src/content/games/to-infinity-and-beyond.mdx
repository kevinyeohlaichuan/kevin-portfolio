Climb as high as possible in 60 seconds. The tower mixes spikes, enemies that
fire straight shots and enemies that launch slower homing projectiles.

The player does not die. Every hit throws them in the opposite direction, so
the challenge is recovering quickly and protecting the height already gained.
A run records the highest climb and player name.
