End-to-end delivery of a browser-based sales experience for a residential
development: buyers explore the towers, filter by floor, inspect individual
units and move through galleries without installing anything.

## What the work involved

The source model arrived at 239.8 MB — unusable over a Malaysian mobile
connection. Rebuilding the asset pipeline through Blender and glTF, with
mesh decimation and texture compression tuned per material, brought the
runtime to 34.6 MB while keeping the silhouette and material reads intact.

The frontend runs on Babylon.js with a React interface layer. Unit data,
availability and content are served from MySQL through PHP, with schema
work done directly in HeidiSQL.

## Where it went

Deployed to production and used as a live sales tool.
