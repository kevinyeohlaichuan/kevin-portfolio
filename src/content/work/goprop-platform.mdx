The showcase experiences answer "what does this development look like".
The platform answers "which development should I be looking at" — verified
listings, location and transit context, and recommendations driven by
stated buyer preferences.

## What the work involved

Full-stack: React product surfaces, 3D property maps for spatial context,
and a Laravel and Node/Fastify backend over MySQL. The 3D layer reuses the
same asset discipline as the individual project showcases, so a development
can move from showcase to marketplace listing without a second pipeline.
